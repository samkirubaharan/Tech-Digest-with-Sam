## Mr & Ms Curious

> **Ms. Curious:** ok so someone just sent me this — "RAG is Dead or is it?" — honestly? I think it's dead

> **Mr. Curious:** it's not dead, you just know Naive RAG. there are versions to this

> **Ms. Curious:** versions?

> **Mr. Curious:** yes — Advanced RAG, Modular RAG, Agentic RAG. each one fixes what the last one got wrong. retrieval got smarter, not obsolete

> **Ms. Curious:** ok but here's the thing — 1M token context windows. why retrieve at all when you can just... put everything in?

> **Mr. Curious:** ...

> **Ms. Curious:** exactly. the models are getting big enough to swallow entire codebases. the whole retrieval problem starts dissolving when the context window is that large

> **Mr. Curious:** that's actually a fair point

> **Ms. Curious:** 1M tokens isn't the ceiling either. that space is moving fast. at some point the architecture question becomes less "what do I retrieve" and more "what do I even need to store separately"

> **Mr. Curious:** so maybe the versions don't matter as much if the window keeps growing

> **Ms. Curious:** that's where I'd put my attention right now
