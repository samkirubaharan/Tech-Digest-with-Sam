## Byline

I spent a full session with Claude Design this week. Here's the unfiltered version — what's genuinely impressive, and what to know before you go in.

**What I found:**

- **Opus 4.7 behaves differently inside Claude Design** — it processes one design at a time. Don't expect the same parallel speed you get in other Claude interfaces. Plan for focused, iterative sessions rather than rapid fire
- **The usage limit is weekly, not a rolling window** — it runs out faster than you'd expect, especially when you're in the flow of iterating on a design. Pace yourself
- **Every iteration triggers a Fork Verifier Agent automatically** — quality control is built in. You're not just getting a raw output; there's a check step happening before Claude surfaces the result
- **Tweaks become live toggle controls on screen** — opacity, icon choices, accent tints. You can adjust them directly on the rendered output without going back to the prompt pane
- **Comment annotations are cheaper than rewriting from the left pane** — if you want a small change, annotate on the design rather than describing it from scratch. Much faster iteration loop

The tool is genuinely impressive. The Fork Verifier detail is the one I keep pointing people to — it's not just "AI generates a design." There's a verification pass that catches quality issues before you see the result. That's the architecture shift that makes this feel more like a workflow tool than a generative toy.

The weekly limit is the thing to watch. Go in with a specific goal rather than experimenting broadly — you'll get better output and not burn through the cap on warm-up iterations.
