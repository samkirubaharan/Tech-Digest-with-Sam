## Trending

### Anthropic and AWS: 5 Gigawatts of Intent

Anthropic just expanded its partnership with Amazon to secure up to 5 gigawatts of new compute for training and deploying Claude. The first capacity comes online this quarter, with nearly 1 gigawatt expected by end of 2026. The 10-year commitment to AWS is valued at over $100 billion. Amazon is also putting in an additional $5 billion today, with up to $20 billion more on the table.

**Why this matters more than the numbers suggest**

Recent decisions from Anthropic had been raising questions. Usage plans were getting tighter. Flagship model access was being restricted. Anthropic CEO Boris Dayma spoke openly in a podcast about the company's conservative token usage strategy while competitors were giving away more at the same price point — which, read between the lines, confirmed that Anthropic was managing genuine compute scarcity.

The string of usage cuts wasn't a product decision. It was an infrastructure constraint.

This announcement is the answer to that. A multi-year, multi-gigawatt compute commitment doesn't flip overnight — but it signals that the bottleneck Anthropic has been quietly managing is now being addressed at a different scale.

**What it could mean for Claude users**

The hope here is straightforward: more compute availability should translate into more headroom for usage plans, and less pressure to keep throttling flagship model access. Whether that materialises quickly or takes quarters to filter through, this at least moves the conversation from scarcity management to capacity expansion.

For teams that shifted to other providers during the restrictions — this is the infrastructure context that was missing from the surface-level product decisions.
