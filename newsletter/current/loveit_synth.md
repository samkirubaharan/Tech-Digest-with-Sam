## Love It!

Anthropic just dropped Claude Design and I have to talk about it — this one genuinely surprised me.

A dedicated tool for creating designs, prototypes, slides, and one-pagers entirely through conversation. No drag-and-drop. No template browsing. Just describe what you want, and Claude builds the first version.

Here's what makes this more than a cool demo:

- **Powered by Claude Opus 4.7 Vision** — actually understands visual context, not just text prompts
- **Reads your codebase and design files** to build your team's design system automatically — every output stays on-brand without manual style enforcement
- Iterate through conversation, inline comments, direct edits, or custom sliders
- Export to Canva, PDF, or PPTX — or hand off directly to Claude Code for implementation
- Available now in research preview on Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans

The part I keep coming back to: it learns your design system from your actual files. That's the move that shifts this from novelty to real workflow change. Brand guidelines in a Figma file or CSS tokens? Claude internalises that and applies it consistently. Most design tools still can't do that.

We're watching the design handoff problem get quietly solved — and I absolutely love it.

Full disclosure: this newsletter is designed using Claude Design. I've been using it personally and it's been a genuinely great experience. Try it at claude.ai/design.
